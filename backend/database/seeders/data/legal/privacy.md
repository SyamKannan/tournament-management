This Privacy Policy explains what personal data KickWick collects, why, who can see it, and the choices you have. It applies to the KickWick website, the installable app and every service on them.

## 1. What we collect

- **Account details** — name, mobile number, email address, password (stored only in scrambled, hashed form) and role.
- **Club details** — club name, address, contact person and contact numbers.
- **Team and player details** — team name and logo; for each player their name, photo, jersey number, playing role, age or date of birth and mobile number, as entered by the player, their team manager or the organizer.
- **Match data** — squads, line-ups, scores, events, statistics and awards.
- **Payments** — amounts, payment method, transaction references and receipts. Card and bank details are handled by our payment partner and never reach us.
- **Messages and support** — WhatsApp and SMS messages sent through the Platform, your opt-out choices, and support tickets with any screenshots you attach.
- **Technical data** — IP address, device and browser type, and sign-in times, used for security, fraud prevention and records of consent.

## 2. Why we use it

- To run the Platform: accounts, registrations, fixtures, live scoring, auctions, receipts and statistics.
- To send messages you would expect: match reminders, registration and payment updates, password reset codes and support replies.
- To keep the Platform secure: to detect misuse and to keep records of payments and of who accepted which terms.
- To bill organizers for their subscriptions.
- To improve KickWick, using aggregated information that does not identify you.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

## 3. What is public

Sports results are public by nature. Tournament, team and player names, photos, jersey numbers, squads, scores, statistics and awards can appear on public tournament pages, scoreboards, posters and player profiles. **Mobile numbers, email addresses, addresses and dates of birth are never shown publicly.**

## 4. Who else sees it

- **Organizers** see the details of teams and players registered in their tournaments, including contact numbers, so that they can run them.
- **Service providers** that operate parts of the Platform for us, only for that purpose:
  - payment processors (such as Razorpay);
  - messaging providers (such as Twilio and Meta WhatsApp);
  - hosting and storage providers;
  - AI providers that power the Scorey assistant — only public tournament data and your question are sent, never contact details.
- **Authorities**, where the law requires it.

## 5. How long we keep it

We keep your data for as long as your account or the tournament records are in use. Payment records, receipts and consent records are kept for as long as tax and other laws require, even after an account is closed. Other data is deleted or anonymised when it is no longer needed.

## 6. Your choices and rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can:

- see and correct your details, from your profile or by asking your organizer;
- opt out of WhatsApp and SMS messages at any time;
- ask us to delete your account and personal data, subject to the records we must keep by law;
- withdraw consent, which may mean you can no longer use parts of the Platform;
- raise a complaint with us, and then with the Data Protection Board of India.

To make a request, write to support@kickwick.com or use Help & Support inside the app.

## 7. Children

Many players are under 18. A child's details should be entered by, or with the consent of, a parent or guardian, and are used only to take part in tournaments. If you believe a child's data was added without consent, contact us and we will remove it.

## 8. Security

Passwords are hashed, connections are encrypted, and each club's data is kept separate from every other club's. No system is perfectly secure, so keep your password private and tell us straight away if you suspect misuse.

## 9. Changes to this policy

When this policy changes in a way that matters, we will ask you to read and accept the new version the next time you sign in. Every earlier version stays available on this page.

## 10. Contact

Privacy questions and requests: support@kickwick.com.
